## 🎯 The Secret to Winning (Top 1% Loom Presentation Rules)
1. **Explain Like You Are Talking to Family**: Avoid dense jargon upfront. Explain *what problem real people face*, *why they get tricked*, and *how your software saves their life savings*.
2. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Always perform the action on screen 1 second before or as you talk about it. Keep your mouse cursor steady and smooth.
3. **Contrast Fake vs Real**: The dramatic comparison between the terrifying fake notice and the benign bank OTP alert is your "aha!" moment for the judges.
4. **Emphasize Architectural Discipline**: Non-technical judges will appreciate that you *didn't blindly trust an AI chatbot to give verdicts*. Explaining *"Rules decide, AI explains"* proves real engineering maturity.
5. **Pacing**: Speak at a comfortable 125–135 words per minute. Do not rush. Let visual elements breathe for 1–2 seconds.

## 📋 Loom Recording Equipment & Staging Checklist
1. **Screen Resolution**: Set display to **1920 × 1080 (16:9 1080p)**. Browser zoom set to **110%** for maximum legibility of Gurmukhi, Devanagari, and score meters.
2. **Webcam Positioning**: Place your face camera in the top-right or bottom-right corner. Maintain eye contact with the lens during the intro (0:00–0:50) and outro (6:30–7:00).
3. **Audio Quality**: External pop-filtered USB microphone. Close windows and turn off fans/AC to eliminate background noise.
4. **Staging Windows**:
   - Window 1: Chrome browser on `http://127.0.0.1:8000` (SachPatra app in English).
   - Window 2: VS Code / Terminal with `python -m pytest -q` command ready to execute.
5. **No Secrets on Screen**: Confirm `.env` files and API keys are never opened or exposed.
