Transparency & How SachPatra Works

        
Open audit: Complete architecture, rules, and privacy commitments.

      


      
      

        

          
⚖️

          
1. Deterministic Rule Engine

          
Verdicts come ONLY from transparent deterministic rules. AI never decides the verdict and cannot hallucinate a scam status.

        


        

          
🔒

          
2. Absolute Privacy

          
Zero content logging. Neither notice texts nor uploaded images are saved to disk or database. Only anonymous counters are kept.

        


        

          
🤖

          
3. AI Only Reads & Explains

          
Multimodal AI is used exclusively as an optional reader for notice images and for simple explanations. If offline, the core rules still work 100%.

        


        

          
🏛️

          
4. Grounded in Law

          
Backed by the Supreme Court of India's landmark suo motu order (17 Oct 2025) confirming digital arrest has no legal authority.
